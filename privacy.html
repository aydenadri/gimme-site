GIMME

Privacy policy. Last updated 2 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. GIMME does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it does not track you.

How the game works

  • The game runs on your device and makes no network requests of its own.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
  • The game does not ask for your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your name, your face and your settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • A match is played by sending messages in a Messages conversation. Each shot you send carries the name and face you chose, the shot and the score, to the people in that conversation. Messages carries it; we never receive it.

Apple

Messages and downloads are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page and its date will change first.
