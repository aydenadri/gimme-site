Playing a match

  • Open Messages and start a conversation with a friend.
  • Tap GIMME in the app drawer, take your shot and send it.
  • Your friend plays the same hole on their phone when they get to it. The hole waits for their reply, so nobody has to be online at the same time.

On your own

Open the GIMME app and tap PRACTICE for a hole with no one waiting.

Your name and face

Set them in SETTINGS. They travel with every shot you send, to the people in that conversation.

Saves

Your name, your face and your settings live on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Connection

GIMME makes no network requests of its own. Sending a shot goes through Messages, which needs a connection.

Sound

If there is no sound, check the silent switch and turn the volume up.
